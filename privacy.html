01

Who we are

The Backbone Ops & Design Inc. ("The Backbone", "we", "us") is a bookkeeping and business administration company incorporated in British Columbia, Canada. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit thebackbone.ca, join our newsletter, contact us or work with us as a client.

We follow British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and, where it applies, the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

02

What we collect

We only collect personal information that you give us, such as:

  • Your email address, when you join our newsletter
  • Your name, email and the details you share when you contact us or book an intro call
  • Business information, financial records, payroll details and documents you share as a client
  • Anything else you choose to include when you write to us

We don't take payments through this website. Agreements and invoices are handled directly with you.

03

How we use it

  • To answer your questions and figure out if we're the right fit
  • To deliver the bookkeeping, payroll and admin services you hired us for
  • To keep you posted about your account, deadlines and deliverables
  • To meet our legal obligations, including CRA, BC Ministry of Finance and WorkSafeBC requirements
  • To send our newsletter, only if you signed up for it

We never sell, rent or trade your personal information.

04

Our newsletter

When you join our newsletter, we use your email to send CRA deadline reminders, tips and your intro call code. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to remove you at any time. We send newsletters in line with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

Our newsletter is delivered through an email service provider, which stores your email address on our behalf and may do so outside of Canada.

05

Our website

Our website doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. To display our fonts, your browser connects to Google Fonts, which can see your IP address the way any website does. Links to Instagram or our booking page take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

06

Who we share it with

We only share personal information when it's needed to do the work, and never for anyone else's marketing:

  • Your accountant or CPA: with your consent, to prepare year-end and tax filings.
  • Government authorities: when we act as your authorized representative, we submit filings and information to the CRA, the BC Ministry of Finance, WorkSafeBC and similar bodies.
  • Service providers: the trusted tools we use to run our business, such as accounting software, email, file storage, scheduling and newsletter services. They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
  • When the law requires it: for example, a court order.

07

Confidentiality

Many of our clients are public figures. Everyone on our team is bound by confidentiality, and we sign NDAs on request. We don't name clients, share their details or use their information in testimonials or marketing without written permission.

08

Storage and security

We keep client information in password-protected systems with two-factor authentication where available, and give access only to the team members who need it for your work.

Some of the tools we use store data outside of Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to local laws. We choose providers with strong security practices.

No system is 100% secure. If a privacy breach ever puts you at real risk of significant harm, we will tell you and report it as the law requires.

09

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it, or as long as the law requires. The CRA requires business records to be kept for six years from the end of the tax year they relate to. We keep client records for seven years to be safe, then securely delete or destroy them.

If you unsubscribe from our newsletter, we remove your email from our mailing list.

10

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong or incomplete
  • Stop using your information, unless we're legally required to keep it
  • Delete your information when we no longer have to keep it

Email us and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for BC.

11

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a change affects how we use information you've already given us, we'll ask for your consent first.
