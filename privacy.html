01

Who we are

The Backbone Ops & Design Inc. ("The Backbone", "we", "us", or "our") is a bookkeeping and business administration company incorporated in British Columbia, Canada. Our office is located at #201 – 15350 Croydon Drive, Surrey, BC.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you visit our website at thebackbone.ca or contact us about our services. We are committed to complying with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

02

Information we collect

We collect personal information only when you voluntarily provide it to us. This may include:

  • Your name and email address when you contact us via email
  • Business name, industry, and details you share when inquiring about our services
  • Financial and bookkeeping information you share as part of an active client engagement
  • Any other information you choose to include in correspondence with us

We do not collect payment information through our website. All service agreements and payments are handled directly via email and invoicing outside of this website.

We also collect anonymized usage data through Google Analytics when you visit our website. See Section 4 for details.

03

How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and assess your service needs
  • Deliver the bookkeeping and administrative services you have engaged us for
  • Communicate with you about your account, deadlines, and deliverables
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations (including CRA, BC Ministry of Finance, and WorkSafeBC requirements)
  • Improve our services and website experience

We do not use your personal information for marketing purposes without your consent, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your information to any third party.

04

Google Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC. Google Analytics uses cookies — small text files placed on your device — to help us understand how visitors interact with our site. This data is anonymized and aggregated; we cannot identify you personally from it.

The information collected includes:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • General geographic location (city/region level only)
  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • How you arrived at our site (e.g. search engine, direct link)

Google may transfer this data to servers in the United States or other countries. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

05

Who we share your information with

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties except in the following limited circumstances:

  • CPA partners: With your consent, we may share your financial records with our vetted CPA network to coordinate year-end tax filings on your behalf.
  • Government authorities: As your authorized representative, we may submit filings and information to CRA, the BC Ministry of Finance, WorkSafeBC, or other regulatory bodies as required to deliver our services.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or court order.

We do not share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties under any circumstances.

06

How we store and protect your information

Client information is stored using password-protected systems. Financial records and documents are handled with care and are not stored longer than necessary for the purpose they were collected.

While we take reasonable precautions to protect your information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to contact us with any security concerns at support@thebackbone.ca.

Our operations are based in Surrey, BC, Canada. Some tools we use (such as Google Analytics) may process data outside of Canada. Where this occurs, we take steps to ensure appropriate protections are in place.

07

Your rights

Under PIPEDA and BC PIPA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Withdraw consent for the collection or use of your information (subject to legal or contractual obligations)
  • Request that we delete your personal information where we are no longer legally required to retain it

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@thebackbone.ca. We will respond within 30 days.

08

Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes it was collected, or as required by law. For active clients, financial records are retained for a minimum of 7 years in accordance with CRA requirements. When records are no longer needed, they are securely deleted or destroyed.

09

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our website or services after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
