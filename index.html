007   Questions

Good to know

Do you work with my accountant or CPA?+

Yes. We keep your books right all year, then hand your accountant a clean, organized year-end package. Less work for them usually means a smaller bill for you. No accountant yet? We'll connect you with one we trust.

I'm behind by a year or more. Can you still help?+

Yes. It's common. You'll get a fixed quote before we start.

Can you deal with the CRA for me?+

Yes. Once you authorize us as your representative, we prepare and file your GST/HST and payroll remittances directly with the CRA.

Will you sign an NDA?+

Yes, as standard.

Do I need to be in Vancouver?+

No. We work online across BC and meet in person in Vancouver, White Rock, South Surrey and on Vancouver Island.
