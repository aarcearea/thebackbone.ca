Behind on your books?

That's a project,
not a retainer.

If you haven't filed in a year — or three — the monthly plans above aren't the right fit. A catch-up is a different scope entirely. We assess how far behind you are, how many years need to be untangled, and quote the full project as a flat engagement.

As a general guide, expect roughly a couple of months of work per year that's unfiled. Two years behind? Around 4 months. Three years, like some of our most significant cases, can mean close to a year of dedicated work. Missing documentation, mixed personal and business finances, and incomplete records can all affect the timeline. We'll tell you exactly what you're looking at in the free consultation, no surprises.

Common Questions

Answers upfront.

No surprises. We'd rather you know exactly what working with Studio Side looks like before you reach out.

I'm years behind — can you still help? +
Yes — and it's more common than you think. Significant catch-ups aren't handled on a monthly retainer, they're scoped as a flat project. We assess everything in the free consultation — how many years, what records exist, what needs to be reconstructed — and give you a clear quote for the full project. Some take weeks, some take longer. Once you're caught up, you move to a monthly plan to stay current.
How does the weekly retainer work? +
$256 CAD per week covers 8 hours of dedicated work at $32/hr. Before we start, we tell you exactly what those hours will be used for. No surprise invoices.
Do I need to sign a long-term contract? +
It depends on the plan. Basic is month-to-month — cancel anytime. Standard requires a 3-month minimum so we can actually build a reliable routine with your books. Full Service is a 6-month commitment given the complexity of managing multiple business structures. Catch-up projects are flat-quoted separately.
I'm on Vancouver Island — can I still use Studio Side? +
Yes — all bookkeeping, admin, and tax services are available remotely. For document digitization, you mail your physical receipts via Canada Post and we handle everything from there. The only thing not available for VI clients is in-person drop-off.
Why does Full Service require a 3-month contract? +
Full Service involves managing books across multiple structures — corporation, sole proprietorship, and personal finances. The setup phase alone takes time: getting full access to all bank statements, building the right categorization structure, and making sure nothing is mixed. Six months ensures we can actually deliver clean, reliable results across all three. We'll walk you through exactly what's needed in your free consultation. We'll walk you through exactly what's needed in your free consultation.
What happens if I get audited? +
We've got you. If CRA reviews your file, we work directly with your CPA to organize your records exactly the way they need them for the audit. You don't have to figure out what to pull together or how to present it — that's our job. Your CPA handles all communication with CRA on your behalf.
Do I still need to find a tax person? +
No. We have a network of trusted Canadian CPAs — including Liberty Tax — who handle annual tax filing for our clients. We prepare your year-end package and connect you with the right professional.
How long does receipt digitization take? +
Standard turnaround is 1 week for up to 200 receipts. If you need it faster, we offer a 48-hour fast-track option at a premium rate — just ask when you reach out and we'll quote you.
How far back can you catch up my books? +
As far back as you need. We've helped clients catch up from 3 months to 3 years behind. There's no judgment — it's more common than you think.
